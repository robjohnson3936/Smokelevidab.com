Shipping

Where we ship

We ship to addresses in the United States, including Alaska, Hawaii and U.S. territories. Some states and cities restrict the sale of these products. Please make sure your purchase is legal where you live.

How it ships

Orders ship from Denver, Colorado by USPS Ground Advantage. Shipping is priced by distance and shown in your order summary before you place your order. Each extra unit in the same order adds $2.00 to shipping.

When it ships

We ship within 2 business days after your Venmo payment arrives. Most packages arrive 2 to 5 business days after that. We'll email you a tracking number when your order ships.

Lost or late packages

If your tracking hasn't updated in 7 days, or it shows delivered but you can't find it, email us and we'll work with USPS to sort it out.

Wholesale orders

Shipping for wholesale orders is quoted with your order.

Returns and refunds

Damaged or defective

If your LEVIDAB arrives damaged or doesn't work as it should, email us within 7 days of delivery with a photo. We'll send a replacement or a full refund, including shipping, at no cost to you.

Changed your mind

You can return an unused LEVIDAB in its original packaging within 30 days of delivery. For hygiene reasons, we can't accept tools that have been used. Return shipping is paid by you, and the original shipping charge isn't refunded.

How to start a return

  • Email smokelevidab@gmail.com with your name and order details.
  • We'll reply with the return address.
  • Once we receive it, we refund you through Venmo within 5 business days.

Wholesale

Returns on wholesale orders follow the terms agreed on your wholesale order.

Privacy

What we collect

When you place an order, we collect your name, email, phone number (if you give it), shipping address, order details and any notes you add. For wholesale orders we also collect your company name.

How we use it

We use your information only to process, ship and support your order, and to contact you about it. We never sell or rent your information.

Services we use

  • Web3Forms delivers your order to our email inbox.
  • Venmo handles your payment under its own privacy policy. We never see your bank or card details.
  • USPS receives your name and address to deliver your package.
  • Vercel hosts this website and counts visits anonymously, without cookies.

Cookies and storage

We don't use tracking cookies. Your browser remembers that you confirmed you're 21 or older, so you aren't asked on every visit.

Keeping and deleting your information

We keep order records for as long as we need them for business and tax purposes. To ask what we have about you, or to have it deleted, email smokelevidab@gmail.com.

Age requirement

LEVIDAB products are for adults 21 and older only. By using this site and placing an order, you confirm you are at least 21 and that buying this product is legal where you live.
