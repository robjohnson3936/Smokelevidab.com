Our Story
A dab tool deserves a place to rest.

LEVIDAB started with a simple problem: there was never a good place to set the dab tool down. Laid on a table, the tip picks up lint and leaves residue behind. Leaned against a jar, it rolls away.

Robert Johnson designed LEVIDAB to fix that. The tool and stand work as one piece. The magnets hold the handle, the tip stays lifted, and the felt base keeps the whole station in one tidy spot.
